<header2> ::: A bit of Theory :::</header2>

In order to be efficient, the Basic Implant must be loaded with one or more Clusters. In fact, these Basic Implants are only the support for the Clusters which give the bonus. There are three kinds of Clusters: Shining ones, Bright ones and finally Faded ones. Shining Clusters are the most powerful ones, Bright ones come second and Faded ones are the weakest, in these proportions:

<highlight>Shining = Bright + Faded</highlight> (to equal QL).
<highlight>Bright = 60% of Shining</highlight> (to equal QL).
<highlight>Faded = 40% of Shining</highlight> (to equal QL).

Every Basic Implant has three free slots. A free slot for every kind of Cluster. Each Cluster can be fitted in only one slot and on only one specific Basic Implant.

For example:

The Cluster <a href='itemref://101561/101562/200'>Multi Ranged Cluster - Faded (Eye)</a> can only be fitted in the Faded slot of an "Eye" implant (<a href='itemref://101101/101102/200'>Basic Eye Implant</a>).

<header2> ::: Now practical class :::</header2>

To combine a Cluster with an Implant (Basic or not, assuming that the targeted slot is free) you just have to find someone with enough Nano Programming skill. If you do the combination yourself, you have to pick up the Cluster and Shift+Right click on the Implant.

<header2> ::: Things to know :::</header2>

You can combine a Cluster of a higher QL than the Implant, but the Cluster cannot be too low, otherwise you won't be able to combine them (tolerance: 14% for a Faded, 16% for a Bright and 18% for a Shining).

Combining a Cluster with an Implant may result in a slightly higher Implant.

The QL of the Clusters combined with an Implant of a given QL is lowered or raised to this QL.

There is a very useful program to help you design your Implants: <a href='chatcmd:///start http://www.timezulu.com.au/NanoNanny/'>Nanonanny</a>.

All Implants require some skill in Treatment, as well as a Secondary Skill (a main attribute) depending on all the Clusters fitted in the Implant. Because of this, it may be helpful to use a not-so-useful Cluster rather than another one, just to make this Secondary Skill deviate to a higher main Attribute, allowing you to put on a higher QL Implant.

Another way to solve this problem is "Implant Stepping". This tactic was changed some patches ago: now the Implant slot in your Wear panel must already be empty before you put on a new Implant. This still allows you to have an intermediary Implant in one slot raising a main Attribute while you put your final Implant in another slot (e.g. you are two points short in Psy to put on your QL60 Head Implant, so you use a QL10 Chest Implant with a Bright Psychic Cluster on it, which gives you +5 Psy). This process still requires you to plan your Implant scheme a bit more...

Fixers can remove Clusters from the Implant, resulting in a Basic Implant of the same QL. Still, during the process, all the Clusters incorporated in the Implant are lost. (Done with the help of an <a href='chatcmd:///tell <myname> recipe 51'>Implant Disassembly Clinic</a>)
